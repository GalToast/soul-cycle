SOUL-CYCLE — QUICKSTART GUIDE
===============================

// WHAT IS THIS?
Procedural life simulation. Live multiple lives across eras (Ancient → Far Future), each ending in death. Accumulate soul fragments that seed your next incarnation.

// CORE MECHANICS (5 ways to interact)

[1] CRISIS DECISIONS
When CRISIS appears, you have 4 seconds to choose:
  A = Intervene (risk death/contagion)
  B = Pray (risk nothing happens)
  C = Watch (do nothing, risk survivor's guilt)
Timer auto-selects Watch. Press key or click.

[2] FRAGMENT WHISPERS
When a past-life fragment whispers, a panel appears at bottom of viewport:
  Y = Act (new event fires, biased by fragment type/tags)
  N = Ignore (whisper fades, no event)
Panel auto-dismisses after 8 seconds.

[3] END LIFE (Tactical Exit)
Click DIE NOW to end the current life early.
If you have resources (Con●○○ Vit●○○ Mom●○○ dots), a confirmation appears — confirming depletes ALL resources to 0.
Resources build through events: connection from relationships, vitality from health, momentum from achievements.

[4] SPEED CONTROLS
SLOW / NORMAL / FAST buttons control event firing speed.
Slow down during crises to think. Speed up during calm.

[5] NEW LIFE
Click NEW LIFE to start a new incarnation.
Soul state (fragments, anchors, unlocks) persists between lives.
New life is rolled from eras available based on your fragment count.

// J-CURVE MORTALITY
The curve shows mortality risk by age. Most die in infancy or old age.
Middle years are dangerous due to war, plague, violence.
Curve animates as you live through events.

// FRAGMENT SYSTEM
On death, 3-5 fragments are extracted: death imprint, relationship memory, emotional tone, memorable event, random whisper.
Fragments with strength >= 0.6 become visible in the pool.
Fragments recurring across 2+ lives become ANCHORS (★) — stronger narrative seeds.
Era unlocks at thresholds: 3=Early Modern, 7=Industrial, 12=Modern, 18=Near Future, 25=Far Future.

// WHAT MAKES IT INTERESTING
- No two lives are the same — era, name, location, events all random
- Crisis decisions meaningfully affect your cascade trajectory
- Fragment whispers give agency over which past-life imprints influence current life
- Life arc chains (GRIEF/LOVE/DREAD/WONDER/RAGE/GRACE) emerge as themes
- Anchors create throughlines — same emotional imprints surfacing in different eras

// QUICK TEST WALKTHROUGH
1. Open index.html in browser
2. Click NEW LIFE
3. Read events, watch the curve
4. If CRISIS appears: pick A/B/C
5. If whisper panel appears: Y or N
6. Let life run, watch resources build
7. Click DIE NOW or wait for natural death
8. Read death card, see fragments collected
9. Click NEW LIFE again — new era/life begins
